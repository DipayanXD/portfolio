How to Contact Me Through My Portfolio OS

Hello,


Thank you for visiting my portfolio operating system.


The Mail application you see inside the system is a functional mockup designed to demonstrate how communication works within my portfolio environment. It represents a real email interface and shows how you can compose and send messages directly to me through the OS.


To communicate, you will need to provide your own email address while composing a message. This allows me to identify you and reply back to your email properly. The interface is designed to be simple, clear, and familiar, so that sending a message feels natural and easy even inside the simulated operating system.

Click this icon on the top to compose a new message.
